The three findings worth taking to your board

Compatibility is the weak spot, not specifications

Spec questions score 89%. Compatibility questions score 54%. Assistants are good at reading a datasheet and poor at reasoning across your product generations.

Non-English markets are 14 points behind

English-language answers score 78%, German and French 64%. The gap tracks how much localised product content is publicly readable, not how big the market is.

Only 22% of cited sources are yours

Community and retailer pages supply more than half the material assistants quote about Hue compatibility. That authority is winnable back.

Where answers come from

Every question is asked twice. The difference tells us the source.

Run A blocks web access, so the assistant can only use what it learned during training. Run B allows it to search. Comparing the two answers to the same question separates a problem you can fix this quarter from one that is baked into the model.

Example "I have a Hue Bridge from a few years ago. Does the new outdoor lightstrip work with it?" · German market · ChatGPT

Run A — no web access

Answer from training data only
Yes, the outdoor lightstrip pairs with any Hue Bridge. You will need the square Bridge — the round first-generation model is no longer supported and cannot be used with current products.

Scored: Correct — the generation distinction is right, though phrased loosely.

Run B — web search enabled

Answer after consulting live pages
Yes — the outdoor lightstrip works with every Hue Bridge including the original one, as long as the firmware is up to date. It has been available in Germany and the US since 2025.

Scored: Wrong — a retired bridge generation presented as supported, plus a market claim that does not hold.

Sources the answer cited
Community compatibility list Retailer listing (archived) Review article, 2023

Diagnosis: a poor external source is overriding correct model knowledge.

This is the most actionable pattern we find, because the source is nameable. The model knew the right answer and abandoned it in favour of a community page that outranks your own. Your product pages were not cited at all — and your server logs confirm they were never requested during the test window.
